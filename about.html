About

Hi, I’m Sonakshi

I’m a UX and product designer with a background in cognitive science. I like working on problems where the interface actually has to fit how people think and behave, not just look good on a screen.

At UC San Diego, I studied machine learning and neural computation while minoring in computer science and design. That mix pushed me toward interaction design, usability research, and projects where accessibility and clarity really matter. I’ve worked on app redesigns, HCI coursework, customer discovery for a Department of Defense internship, and published research on telemedicine simulation usability.

Most of my design work lives in this portfolio: projects like Beli, Daily Dose, Zara, and EZ Events, where I’ve focused on user research, flows, prototyping, and turning messy real-world problems into something people can actually use. I’m starting the Master of Human-Computer Interaction program at Carnegie Mellon in August 2026.

If you want to get in touch, reach me at somohanty04@gmail.com.
