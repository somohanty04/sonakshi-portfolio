Hybrid Usability Analysis to Improve an Educational Telemedicine Simulation: Task Analysis and Survey Results

Lesselroth B, Monkman H, Marcilly R, Mohanty S, Anderson M, Parsons C, Bellavia K, Foulks K, Lawson A, Homco J, Richards M, Gold K.

Studies in Health Technology and Informatics. 2025 May 12;326:59–63.

doi: 10.3233/SHTI250237 · PMID: 40357602

Hybrid usability study combining task analysis and survey methods to refine an educational telemedicine simulation—evaluating how clinicians and learners interact with the system and where the experience breaks down.
